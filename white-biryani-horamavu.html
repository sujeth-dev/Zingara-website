The Best White Biryani in Horamavu

Mysore style. 19 spices. Dum cooked, every single day.

Ask around Horamavu — or “Hormavu,” as it’s just as often typed — and biryani is never far from the conversation. It’s one of the most competitive food categories in this stretch of north-east Bengaluru, with new counters and cloud kitchens opening every few months. Most of them serve the same thing: an orange-red biryani coloured with food dye, cooked fast, tasting of masala rather than spice. Zingara does the opposite. Our biryani is white, not because we’re withholding colour, but because we never added any to begin with. What you see is milk, ghee and jeera rice, layered over chicken or mutton that has taken on 19 hand-ground spices through slow, sealed dum cooking rather than a shortcut sauce.

This is the Mysore style of white biryani — a tradition that predates the red biryani most of Bangalore has grown used to. It relies on restraint: no synthetic colour, no MSG, no pre-mixed masala packets. Every batch is cooked fresh each morning in small quantities, which is also why Zingara tends to sell out before closing — something regulars in Horamavu have come to expect and plan around.

Find Us

Where to find Zingara in Horamavu

Zingara sits inside Horamavu, the residential neighbourhood in Bengaluru’s BBMP east zone bordered by Ramamurthy Nagar, Banaswadi, Kalkere and Kalyan Nagar. If you’re orienting yourself by landmark rather than address, think Horamavu Main Road, or the walking track around Horamavu Agara Lake and Horamavu Lake — both a short drive from the restaurant. The area sits just off the Outer Ring Road corridor that runs toward Manyata Tech Park, which is why a lot of our weekday orders come in from people finishing work nearby.
