Biryani Delivery to TC Palya

Mysore style white biryani, from Horamavu to your door.

TC Palya — written out in full as T C Palya, and locally sometimes as Thirumenahalli Cross Palya — is the road and junction that connects Horamavu to Jayanthi Nagar and the Banaswadi side of this part of Bengaluru. It falls administratively within the Horamavu ward, which is also why a lot of people searching for food near TC Palya end up finding kitchens based in Horamavu proper — Zingara among them.

We cook one thing well: Mysore style white biryani, built on a 19 spice blend that’s ground fresh each morning and never touched with artificial colour. No red dye, no shortcuts — just milk, ghee, jeera rice and a slow, sealed dum cook. It’s a different biryani from what most delivery apps show first, and it tends to win over TC Palya regulars who try it once.

Directions

Getting to Zingara from TC Palya

From the TC Palya Main Road junction, head toward Horamavu Main Road — Zingara is a short drive in, on the Horamavu side rather than the Jayanthi Nagar side. It’s a straightforward route along the main stretch, without much need to navigate narrow internal lanes.

Delivery

Ordering from TC Palya

Delivery to TC Palya generally takes about 20–30 minutes once your order is confirmed, similar to the rest of Horamavu — a little longer if you’re closer to the Jayanthi Nagar end of the road. We cook fresh, small batches through the day, so biryani can sell out before closing; ordering earlier in the evening is the safer bet if you have a specific dish in mind.

You can order through Swiggy or Zomato, or reach us directly on WhatsApp for pickup or to check what’s still available.

The Area

Horamavu, Jayanthi Nagar and the TC Palya stretch

TC Palya Main Road runs through a mix of residential layouts and small businesses, linking the Horamavu side of the ward to Jayanthi Nagar and on toward Banaswadi. It’s a well-travelled stretch rather than a quiet backstreet, which is partly why delivery here is quick and consistent — riders know the road well.

If you’re closer to Jayanthi Nagar than to Horamavu proper, the same applies: Zingara still delivers, it just adds a few extra minutes to the estimate above. Either way, it’s the same white biryani, cooked the same way, every morning.
